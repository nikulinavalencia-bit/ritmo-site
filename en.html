How it works

Four steps, and nobody does three of them.

  1. A document arrivesA photo of the delivery note, or a chat message: “Waste: tomato 2 kg”.
  2. Ritmo reads itSupplier, number, date, lines, VAT rates and the totals in the footer.
  3. It matchesEach line is linked to your own item. Whatever you fix once is remembered.
  4. It gets postedTwo clicks: review and confirm. Or it goes on its own once everything adds up.

What Ritmo OPS does

📄 Delivery notes and invoices

Photo, PDF or chat message. Reads lines, per-line and global discounts, and reconciles the footer even with several VAT rates.

🧮 VAT that adds up

If the total doesn't match the document, Ritmo says so and picks the 4 / 10 / 21 rates that make it match. Packaging taxes are kept separate.

🔗 Catalogue memory

The supplier's wording is linked to your item once, and the rest of their notes fill in by themselves.

🗑 Write-off acts

Waste, staff meals and tastings are written in the chat as usual. One act per location per day, with its write-off account.

🚚 Transfers between locations

One message creates the outgoing note at the sender and the incoming one at the receiver — at cost or with a markup.

🥖 Production acts

The bakery writes what it baked: ingredients are deducted by recipe and the finished goods are booked in.
